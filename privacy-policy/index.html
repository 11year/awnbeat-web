Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 9, 2026

Awnbeat, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Awnbeat,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you use the Awnbeat mobile application, website, public event pages, communities, messaging features, events, payment features, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

The Service is intended only for individuals who are at least 18 years old and located in the United States.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information from you, automatically through the Service and your device, from other users, and from service providers and connected services.

A. Account and identity information

We may collect:

  • Your name, display name, phone number, email address, full date of birth, city, profile photo, biography, activity interests, social profile links, and account preferences.
  • User IDs, authentication records, verification codes, session information, Sign in with Apple identifiers, and account-security information.
  • Age and eligibility information used to confirm that you are at least 18 years old.
  • Identity-verification information for Hosts, including legal name, date of birth, government-issued identification information or images, identity-verification results, and related records. Stripe may collect some of this information directly and provide Awnbeat with verification status or limited verification details.

B. Contacts and invitations

If you grant Awnbeat access to your device contacts, we may upload contact names, phone numbers, email addresses, and related contact-book information to:

  • Identify contacts who may already use Awnbeat.
  • Connect phone numbers with existing Awnbeat accounts.
  • Help you choose individual people to invite to events or communities.
  • Prevent duplicate or abusive invitations.

Contact access is optional and can be revoked through your device settings. Awnbeat does not sell contact information. Contact information belonging to people who do not use Awnbeat is deleted after matching or within 30 days, unless it is needed for an invitation, guest RSVP, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or another purpose described in this Policy.

You are responsible for having permission to submit another person’s information and for sending invitations only to people you reasonably believe want to receive them.

C. Profile, social, and relationship information

We may collect:

  • Profile visibility and privacy settings.
  • Followers, following, friend requests, community memberships, blocked users, and other social relationships.
  • Verification status and badges.
  • Information about profiles, communities, events, and accounts you follow, join, save, hide, mute, or block.

D. User content and communications

We collect content you create, upload, send, or submit, including:

  • Posts, captions, comments, reactions, likes, tags, reviews, event updates, and community-feed content.
  • Photos, videos, profile images, event images, community images, and message attachments.
  • Direct messages, event chats, community chats, mentions, and related message metadata.
  • Content moderation information, including automated or manual review results.
  • Reports, report reasons, supporting information, enforcement decisions, and appeal information.
  • Support requests, feedback, survey responses, and communications with Awnbeat.

Messages and private content are not public, but Awnbeat may access, preserve, or review them when reasonably necessary to operate the Service, investigate reports, enforce our Terms, protect users, prevent fraud or abuse, respond to emergencies, or comply with law.

E. Events, communities, RSVPs, and attendance

We may collect:

  • Events you create, host, view, join, purchase, save, share, or invite others to.
  • Event details such as title, description, category, media, price, currency, taxes, capacity, team setup, class schedule, start and end times, time zone, location, address, city, state, coordinates, waitlist status, public links, and event status.
  • RSVP, registration, invitation, waitlist, purchase, check-in, attendance, cancellation, no-show, and refund information.
  • Communities you create, manage, join, follow, or request to join, including community content, membership, ownership, administration, and moderation information.
  • Your event check-in status and past attendance locations. Visibility of this information may depend on your account settings and the applicable event or community.

F. Guest RSVP information

People may be able to RSVP to certain events without creating an Awnbeat account. We may collect a guest’s:

  • Name and phone number.
  • Event and RSVP status.
  • Guest count and notes.
  • Check-in, attendance, or no-show status.
  • Invitation source and related timestamps.

If a guest later creates an Awnbeat account using the same phone number, we may connect the guest RSVP to that account.

G. Location information

With your permission, we may collect precise or approximate device location while you use the Service. We use location information to:

  • Display nearby events, communities, and activity locations.
  • Calculate distance and provide location-based search results.
  • Help you select an event, community, or check-in location.
  • Support maps, directions, event discovery, safety, fraud prevention, and location-based features.

We also collect locations you enter or generate through use of the Service, including your city, manually selected location, saved locations, search locations, venue addresses, event coordinates, community locations, check-ins, and attendance locations.

Other users may see your city, event check-in status, or past event attendance based on your privacy settings and the design of the applicable event or community. Awnbeat does not intentionally display your live precise device location to other users.

You can revoke location permission through device settings, but some location-based features may stop working.

H. Calendar information

If you grant calendar permission, Awnbeat may add event details to your device calendar, including the event title, date, time, location, notes, and related information. Calendar access is optional and can be revoked through device settings.

I. Apple Health and fitness information

If you grant Apple Health permission, Awnbeat may read supported workout information so that you can create an update post from a workout. Depending on the feature, this may include workout type, start date, duration, and distance.

Awnbeat:

  • Does not write information to Apple Health.
  • Does not use Apple Health information for advertising, marketing, or unrelated profiling.
  • Does not sell Apple Health information.
  • Does not send Apple Health information to advertising platforms.
  • Uses Apple Health information only for health or fitness features requested by you, Service security, legal compliance, or as otherwise permitted by Apple and applicable law.

Raw Apple Health information used to create a post is deleted after the requested post is created unless you affirmatively save it or the information must be retained for security, legal, or dispute purposes. Information you choose to publish in a post becomes User Content and may remain visible according to your selected audience until deleted.

Revoking Apple Health permission stops future access but does not automatically delete posts you previously created from workout information.

J. Photos, camera, and videos

If you grant permission or choose media through a system picker, we may access and process selected photos and videos. We may resize, compress, transcode, scan, store, review, or moderate media to provide the Service, enforce our rules, protect users, and comply with law.

K. Payments, fees, refunds, verification, and payouts

Awnbeat uses Stripe and Stripe Connect for paid-event transactions, identity verification, tax-related functions, refunds, disputes, Host onboarding, and payouts.

We may collect or receive:

  • Purchase history, amounts, currency, taxes, platform fees, processing fees, Host fees, refunds, credits, disputes, chargebacks, payout records, reserves, adjustments, and transaction timestamps.
  • Stripe customer IDs, PaymentIntent IDs, charge IDs, checkout session IDs, Connected Account IDs, transfer IDs, payout IDs, receipt links, and related metadata.
  • Host legal name, date of birth, verification status, onboarding status, payout eligibility, account type, country, currency, charges-enabled status, payouts-enabled status, and tax-document status.
  • Limited payment-method details such as brand, last four digits, and expiration information where supplied by Stripe.

Stripe may directly collect full payment card numbers, bank account information, government identification, tax information, identity-verification images, and fraud-prevention information under Stripe’s own terms and privacy policy. Awnbeat generally does not receive full card or bank account numbers.

L. Device, notification, analytics, diagnostic, and cookie information

We may automatically collect:

  • Device type, operating system, app version, language, time zone, IP address, device or app identifiers, and network information.
  • Apple Push Notification service tokens, notification preferences, delivery status, badge counts, and notification interactions.
  • Searches, screens viewed, taps, saves, follows, joins, RSVPs, checkout attempts, feature usage, referral source, and other interactions.
  • Crash reports, performance information, errors, security logs, fraud signals, and server request information.
  • Browser and website information collected through cookies, local storage, pixels, or similar technologies.

We use PostHog for product analytics and may use hosting, diagnostic, crash-reporting, customer-support, communication, and security providers to process this information on our behalf.

You may control cookies through your browser and certain analytics or notification choices through the app or device settings. Disabling these technologies may affect Service functionality.

2. Sources of Information

We collect information:

  • Directly from you.
  • Automatically from your device, browser, and use of the Service.
  • From Hosts, invite senders, event attendees, community administrators, and other users.
  • From Apple, Stripe, Supabase, PostHog, Typeform, and other service providers or connected services.
  • From publicly available sources where permitted by law.
  • From fraud-prevention, identity-verification, moderation, safety, and security providers.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, personalize, and improve the Service.
  • Create, verify, secure, and administer accounts.
  • Confirm that users meet the Service’s 18-and-older eligibility requirement.
  • Display profiles, posts, events, communities, maps, activity locations, messages, search results, and recommendations.
  • Match contacts to accounts and support user-initiated invitations.
  • Process RSVPs, purchases, classes, waitlists, check-ins, attendance records, cancellations, refunds, disputes, Host fees, taxes, and payouts.
  • Verify Hosts and administer Stripe Connect onboarding.
  • Send authentication codes, receipts, event reminders, safety messages, account notices, and other transactional communications.
  • Send marketing communications when you separately consent, subject to your right to opt out.
  • Moderate content, investigate reports, enforce our Terms, and remove prohibited or unlawful content.
  • Detect, investigate, prevent, and address fraud, spam, abuse, harassment, unsafe behavior, payment risk, security incidents, and violations of law.
  • Provide customer support and respond to privacy, legal, or safety requests.
  • Perform analytics, debugging, testing, research, and product development.
  • Protect Awnbeat, our users, Hosts, venues, service providers, and the public.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, payment-network, law-enforcement, and safety obligations.
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

4. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose personal information as follows.

A. Other users and the public

Depending on your settings and use of the Service, others may see:

  • Your profile, photo, name, biography, city, interests, social links, verification status, followers, and following.
  • Public posts, comments, reactions, videos, events, communities, and event links.
  • Community membership and event participation.
  • Event check-in status and past attendance information, subject to applicable privacy controls.
  • Content you send to message or chat participants.

Public content may be viewed, copied, recorded, or reshared by others outside Awnbeat. Awnbeat cannot control information after another person lawfully receives or copies it.

B. Hosts, community administrators, and event organizers

We may disclose registration, guest RSVP, contact, attendance, check-in, refund, report, and safety information to the relevant Host, community owner, administrator, moderator, or event organizer when reasonably necessary to operate or protect an event or community.

C. Service providers

We disclose information to vendors that help provide the Service, including:

  • Supabase and other cloud-hosting, database, authentication, storage, and infrastructure providers.
  • Stripe and financial, identity-verification, tax, fraud-prevention, and payment partners.
  • Apple services, including Sign in with Apple, Apple Health, Calendar, MapKit, and push notifications.
  • PostHog and analytics providers.
  • Typeform and customer-support, survey, or feedback providers.
  • Email, SMS, notification, media-processing, content-moderation, security, and crash-reporting providers.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, accountants, and insurers.

We require service providers that process personal information on our behalf to use it only for authorized purposes, maintain appropriate safeguards, and provide protections consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

D. At your direction

We may disclose information when you connect a service, share content, send an invitation, add an event to a calendar, complete a payment, publish a workout post, or otherwise direct us to disclose it.

E. Legal, safety, and enforcement purposes

We may preserve or disclose information when we reasonably believe it is necessary to:

  • Comply with law, court orders, subpoenas, legal process, regulatory requests, or payment-network requirements.
  • Respond to emergencies or protect a person from serious harm.
  • Detect, prevent, investigate, or address fraud, abuse, exploitation, harassment, unlawful content, security incidents, or Terms violations.
  • Protect the rights, property, security, and safety of Awnbeat, users, Hosts, service providers, venues, or the public.
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

F. Business transactions

Information may be disclosed or transferred in connection with financing, due diligence, a merger, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, sale of assets, or a similar business transaction, subject to applicable law.

5. Sale, Sharing, and Targeted Advertising

Awnbeat does not sell personal information for money and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Awnbeat does not use Apple Health information, precise location, private messages, government identification, contact-book information, or payment information for targeted advertising.

If our practices change, we will update this Policy and provide any legally required notice and choice before using information in the new manner.

6. Communications

Transactional communications

By creating an account or using event and payment features, you authorize Awnbeat to send communications reasonably necessary to provide the Service, including verification codes, security alerts, receipts, RSVP updates, event reminders, payment notices, refund notices, and support messages.

Marketing emails and text messages

We may send marketing emails or text messages only where you have provided any consent required by law. Consent to marketing texts is not a condition of purchase or use of the Service. Message frequency may vary, and message and data rates may apply.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe link. You can opt out of marketing texts by replying STOP. You may reply HELP for help. Opting out of marketing messages does not stop necessary transactional communications.

Invitations

Event or community invitations may be sent at a user’s direction. Recipients may contact us at info@awnbeat.com to request that Awnbeat stop sending invitations to their phone number or email address.

7. Your Choices and Controls

Depending on the feature, you may:

  • Edit profile information and privacy settings.
  • Control whether attendance information is shown through available account or event settings.
  • Delete posts, media, messages, events, communities, or other content where deletion is supported.
  • Leave communities, cancel RSVPs, block users, and submit reports.
  • Manage push-notification preferences.
  • Revoke location, contacts, calendar, camera, photos, and Apple Health permissions through device settings.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.
  • Delete your account through the app.
  • Submit a privacy request as described below.

Revoking permission stops future collection through that permission but may not delete information already collected or content you chose to publish.

8. Account Deletion

You may initiate deletion of your Awnbeat account through the app’s account settings.

Account deletion removes or de-identifies your profile, posts, uploaded media, messages, RSVPs, community relationships, push tokens, contact-matching records, Apple Health information stored by Awnbeat, and other account-linked information, generally within 30 days.

When necessary, we may retain limited information for tax, accounting, payment, fraud-prevention, security, safety, content-moderation, dispute-resolution, legal-compliance, and enforcement purposes. We may also retain records that involve other users, such as transaction records, reports, messages received by others, and event records necessary to document a purchase or incident.

Events or communities you created may be cancelled, transferred, anonymized, or retained in limited form when reasonably necessary to protect attendees, complete refunds, preserve records, or maintain other users’ access.

Information may remain in encrypted or restricted backups until the normal backup cycle completes, generally within 90 days.

9. Data Retention

Unless a longer period is required or reasonably necessary for law, safety, security, fraud prevention, disputes, or enforcement, we generally retain information as follows:

  • Active account and profile information: while the account remains active.
  • Deleted-account information: deleted or de-identified within 30 days.
  • Backups: deleted or overwritten through the normal backup cycle, generally within 90 days.
  • Payment, payout, tax, and accounting records: up to 7 years.
  • Security and technical logs: up to 12 months.
  • Guest RSVP records: up to 24 months after the event.
  • Contact information belonging to nonusers: deleted after matching or within 30 days, unless needed for an invitation, RSVP, suppression request, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.
  • Reports, blocks, fraud records, and serious safety or moderation records: up to 5 years, or longer when reasonably necessary.
  • Raw Apple Health information: deleted after the requested post is created unless affirmatively saved or legally required.
  • Deleted public content: removed from active display promptly but may remain temporarily in backups, safety records, or legally required records.

We may retain de-identified or aggregated information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.

10. Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and subject to applicable exceptions, you may have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we process your personal information.
  • Access or obtain a copy of personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete personal information.
  • Obtain certain information in a portable format.
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising, or certain profiling.
  • Appeal a denial of a privacy request.
  • Use an authorized agent where permitted by law.
  • Receive equal service and pricing without unlawful discrimination for exercising privacy rights.

Awnbeat does not currently sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To submit a request, email info@awnbeat.com with the subject line Privacy Request and describe the request. We may need to verify your identity and authority before responding.

We generally respond to verified requests within 45 days where required by law. We may extend the response period when permitted and will explain the reason. If we deny a request, you may appeal by emailing info@awnbeat.com with the subject line Privacy Appeal. We will respond to an appeal within the period required by applicable law.

11. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, including access controls, authentication measures, monitoring, vendor-management practices, and encryption where appropriate.

No security method is completely reliable. You are responsible for protecting your device, phone number, Apple account, email account, and authentication methods and for promptly notifying us if you suspect unauthorized account access.

12. Adults Only

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18. You may not create an account or use the Service if you are under 18.

We collect full date of birth and may use Apple age-assurance information or other verification methods to enforce this requirement. If we learn that a person under 18 has created or used an account, we may suspend or delete the account and associated information, subject to legally required retention.

If you believe a person under 18 is using Awnbeat, contact info@awnbeat.com.

13. United States Service

The Service is offered for use in the United States. We do not intentionally offer the Service to people located outside the United States.

Information may be processed in the United States or in other countries where our service providers operate. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Our service providers are required to process information in accordance with their contracts and applicable law.

14. Third-Party Services and Links

The Service may integrate with or link to third-party services, including Apple services, Supabase, Stripe, PostHog, Typeform, maps, social networks, venue pages, and external websites.

Third parties have their own terms and privacy practices. This Policy does not govern information a third party collects directly from you under its own notice. Review the privacy information provided by the applicable third party.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service or law changes. We will post the revised Policy and update the effective date. If changes materially affect your rights or how we use personal information, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent when required.
